Playbook-driven review: clause comparison, risk flagging, fallback positions, suggested redlines, escalation rules.

Key capabilities:
- Continuous monitoring with explainable AI findings and risk impact
- One-click playbooks routed to your CLM/e-sign stack
- Weekly compliance digest with review throughput and risk totals
- Connects to the rest of the Zion App Network for end-to-end compliance operations
